Privacy Policy

This page explains how the AUTO FINANCE website handles information when you use this static website or contact us using the available call and WhatsApp links.

Information you choose to provide

If you enter your name, phone number, vehicle/budget details or message in the enquiry form, the information is used only to prepare a WhatsApp message on your device. This static page does not include a website database or server-side form processor.

Calls and WhatsApp

When you call us or continue to WhatsApp, the relevant telecom or messaging service may process information according to its own terms and privacy practices. Information you send through those services may be used to respond to your enquiry and assist with finance-related follow-up.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not include advertising pixels, analytics scripts or tracking cookies by default. If the website is later modified to add such tools, this policy should be updated before publishing.

Hosting information

The hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser information and request logs for security and service operation.

Finance applications

If you proceed with a loan enquiry or application, additional personal and financial information may be requested separately for eligibility checks, documentation, lender verification and processing. Applicable lenders or service providers may have their own privacy notices.

Contact

For privacy-related questions about this website, contact AUTO FINANCE at 7736150713.
